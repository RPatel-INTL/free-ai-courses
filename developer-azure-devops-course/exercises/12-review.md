# Exercise 12 · Answer with evidence

**Prerequisite:** [lesson 12](../lessons/12-review.md)'s real human review.

## Your task

1. Restate a genuine review question in your own words.
2. Respond with a focused code/test change and its verified result, or a reasoned explanation supported by source/tests.
3. If changed, stage only actual paths, commit with `#ID`, and push only after specific consent.
4. Obtain the reviewer's recorded approval and inspect required policies.
5. After authorized completion, update local `main` and run `mvn -B verify` in `starter/`.
6. Check the completed PR/work-item link and actual work-item state.

**Independent challenge:** explain why a resolved conversation, a model's positive review, and a required human approval are three different things. State which controls governed this actual merge.

## STOP and evidence

Provide thread/response, real reviewer vote, required-policy status, completed PR, main commit, and verification summary.

## Recovery

Leave the PR pending if review/policy is incomplete. A follow-up commit may invalidate an earlier approval.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Review is genuine, not simulated approval.
- [ ] No required policy was bypassed.
- [ ] Updated `main` contains the reviewed feature and verifies.
- [ ] Absent initial CI policy is explicitly recorded, not claimed as enforced.
